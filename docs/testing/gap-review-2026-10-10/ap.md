## Top 5 for this area

1. **AP-01: held or failed bills are stranded.** No re-match, accept, edit or delete after leaving the entry screen, and the duplicate-invoice guard blocks re-entry. This is the "can't finish the job" dead end.
2. **AP-04 + AP-11: credit purchases don't show as owed, and there's no all-suppliers creditors / due-this-week view.** These are the owner's two most basic questions ("what do I owe, and what's due?"), and neither is answerable today.
3. **AP-07 + AP-08 + AP-02: payments don't match how shops pay.** There's no part-payment on the main screen, the account is always the default, and there's no advance to a brewery. The cash book and supplier balances drift from reality.
4. **AP-05 + AP-06 + AP-14 + AP-15: payables and profit can be corrupted.** The same GRN can be billed twice, the over-billing check compares cartons to bottles, a debit note can hit another supplier's or another currency's bill, and returns inflate profit.
5. **AP-09 + AP-10: payment terms are lost.** Due dates default to today and editing a supplier wipes its terms, so ageing is meaningless. These are cheap fixes (S each).
