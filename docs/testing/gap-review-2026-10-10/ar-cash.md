## Top 5 for this area

1. **ARC-01**: cash counts double-book the day's sales as "cash over" income, so cash and profit are both overstated. Fix the expected-cash formula or block counts on the sales cash account before anyone uses this.
2. **ARC-02 + ARC-03**: the receipt allocation grid, Receivables filters and Receipts filter ignore the chosen customer, and credit notes always fail. These are one-line param mismatches in the core debt-collection screens, small fixes.
3. **ARC-04 + ARC-06 + ARC-11**: there is no undo, apply or refund on a customer receipt. A wrong receipt, a deposit or an over-payment cannot be fixed. The service code for reallocate and the reversal pattern already exist.
4. **ARC-07**: credit hold/stop cannot be set and is not enforced on counter or POS invoices. This is the #1 control a liquor wholesaler selling on credit to bars needs, and the columns already exist.
5. **ARC-08 + ARC-09 + ARC-10**: the cash side doesn't add up. Sales never reach the cash book, the bank reconciliation fails from month 2, and petty-cash expenses never reach the P&L. There is no trustworthy "cash in the shop today" or monthly bank reconciliation.
