## Top 5 for this area
1. **SAL-01**: pack/carton orders invoiced at base qty × carton price, overcharging 24×. It hits Kilimanjaro's carton wholesale directly.
2. **SAL-02**: there's no return or refund path for counter invoices or closed-session POS sales, and every shop needs one daily.
3. **SAL-03 / SAL-06**: voided credit invoices stay owed in AR, and counter deposits on credit sales never reach Cash, so the debtors ledger and GL disagree.
4. **SAL-04 / SAL-05**: pricing ignores price lists (no wholesale vs retail price), and the order route lets anyone set any price, including zero.
5. **SAL-10 / SAL-12 / SAL-13 / SAL-14**: basic counter usability. A broken status filter and customer search, no line edit, no draft cancel, and a 15-step cash sale.
