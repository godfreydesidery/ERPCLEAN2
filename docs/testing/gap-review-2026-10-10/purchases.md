## Top 5 for this area

1. **PUR-01**: the receive-against-PO screen shows pieces under a "Carton" heading and prefills pieces. Every carton LPO either fails with "Over-receipt" or silently books the wrong stock. A one-file web fix.
2. **PUR-02 / PUR-03 / PUR-09**: purchase returns are wrong in three ways. They are valued at carton cost × pieces, can double-reduce stock alongside a GRN void, and post at the user's branch instead of the GRN's. All three are small service fixes.
3. **PUR-06 + PUR-04**: the 3-way match does not hold. Pack-unit over-billing passes, the same GRN can be billed twice, and a billed GRN can be voided.
4. **PUR-05**: approval can be bypassed by adding lines after approval. Small fix, big control gap for the owner.
5. **PUR-12 / PUR-17 / PUR-18**: missing basics an SME expects from Tally or a local POS. There is no margin/selling-price prompt on cost changes, no one-step cash purchase, and no quick-add supplier at the receiving counter.
