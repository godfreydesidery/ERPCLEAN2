Notes:
- **PO approval is not a blocker for a one-owner shop.** `purchase-settings` has `poApprovalEnabled:false` by default, so DRAFT -> `place` -> ORDERED.
- **Things that worked:** direct GRN without LPO (carton -> 24 bottles correct), the moving average on the second LPO (1,833.33), GRN void of a non-billed receipt (stock and average restored), part-payment, the supplier statement PDF, stock value per branch (431,553.88 + 161,027.57 = valuation 592,581.45), and GL journals for GRN, return, bill, payment, adjustment and cash expense. **Transfers post no GL entry** (the Inventory account is company-wide), which is acceptable.
- **Unconfirmed (tested as root only):** a transfer was received while X-Branch-Uid was the source branch (Head Office). Re-test as a non-root user to see whether `findAndAssertScope` restricts receive to the destination branch.

## Top 5
1. **LBO-01/02:** purchase returns of pack units post 1 bottle per crate and accept returns up to 25x the receipt. Stock qty, average cost and the AP debit note are all wrong. One-line fix in `PurchaseReturnServiceImpl.java:131/147`.
2. **LBO-03:** every stock count posts its variance to the GL twice (`StockCountServiceImpl.java:293` + `InventoryValuationServiceImpl.java:551`), so the GL and the stock subledger drift apart every month-end.
3. **LBO-04:** a billed (or returned) GRN can still be voided. AP keeps owing for stock that is gone (`GoodsReceiptServiceImpl.java:263`).
4. **LBO-05 + LBO-07 + LBO-08:** crate/bottle mix-ups in daily purchasing. The Receive screen pre-fills bottles into a crate field ("receive all" always 409s), bill matching compares crates with bottles (over-billing passes), and the printed GRN margin reads -1,700%.
5. **LBO-06 + LBO-11:** after any branch transfer the receiving branch can't adjust that product ("held at more than one location"), and transfer receipt can't record a shortage. Branch-to-branch stock control breaks on day one.
