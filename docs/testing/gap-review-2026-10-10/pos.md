## Top 5 for this area
1. **POS-01 Offline selling.** The till is dead whenever the connection is. Ship a local catalogue plus an offline queue (the idempotency and stale-replay plumbing already exists), or move unstable shops to the LAN server.
2. **POS-02 / POS-05 / POS-04 Drawer controls.** A "Refund" payout with no GL entry and no approval, paid-outs with no approval, and a cashier who can see expected cash together make shrinkage invisible. These are mostly small fixes (hide REFUND, step-up on payouts, hide Expected).
3. **POS-07 Discount/VAT preview mismatch.** The screen total differs from the receipt on every discounted line under VAT-exclusive pricing, and it breaks exact M-Pesa payments.
4. **POS-08 + POS-09 + POS-12 Wholesale and payments.** No on-account sale, no M-Pesa/Tigo/card reference or per-wallet GL account, and price-list-blind pricing. The data fields (`defaultPriceListId`, `isDefault`, `cashBankAccountId`, `mobileMoneyRef`) already exist and are unused.
5. **POS-06 + POS-13 + POS-23 Cashier speed.** No park/recall, 3 or more round-trips per scan plus a stock query per keystroke, and no keyboard shortcuts.
