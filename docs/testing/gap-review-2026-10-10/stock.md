## Top 5 for this area

1. **STK-01**: item-wise adjustment, bulk stock import and opening balance are refused for any product that has passed through a transfer (the zero-qty In-Transit row). The error points to a screen that doesn't exist. Kilimanjaro will hit this daily. It's an S-sized fix.
2. **STK-02**: stocktake posts against live stock, so every sale between counting and posting comes back as phantom stock and phantom GL gain. The "freeze" freezes nothing.
3. **STK-06 + STK-07**: transit, damaged and van stock all count as sellable at the branch, and the transit location can be hijacked by any "Other"-type location a user creates. Together they make inter-branch stock figures untrustworthy.
4. **STK-03 + STK-04 + STK-05**: transfers have no short/broken receipt, no recall after dispatch, and no branch check on who receives. A storekeeper can confirm his own delivery to another branch.
5. **STK-08**: there are no cartons on adjust, count or opening screens (base units only). This is the root of the "daily adjustments" complaint for a carton/bottle liquor business. The unit-conversion code already exists on transfers and can be reused.
