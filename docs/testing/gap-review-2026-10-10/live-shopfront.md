# Live shop-front test — liquor store go-live + one trading day (API :8091, 2026-10-10)

Scripts, raw call log (`out/calls.log`, 437 calls), PDFs and report JSON are in `scratchpad/live-shopfront/`. All calls returned in under 0.3 s (the first `/v3/api-docs` took 9 s on a cold start). There were no 5xx responses. A second tester shared the DB and also rang sales on my SF- products and walk-in customer (INV-0006/0007, a GRN, an adjustment). Their numbers are separated out below.

## Step count

- **One sellable bottle item (price, barcode, stock, valued): 7 API calls on 3 screens.** One-off prerequisite: create a price list (1 call; none exists on a fresh install). Product Master: create product, set price, add barcode, assign branch, opening balance (5 calls). Opening Valuation screen: look up the on-hand uid, then set the opening cost (2 calls).
- **The same item sold per bottle and per crate, retail and wholesale: 12 calls.** On top of the 7: wholesale bottle price, crate bulk pack, crate barcode (broken, see LSF-03), and the crate retail and wholesale prices (Product Detail screen, 2 calls). That is 3 screens (Product Master, Product Detail, Opening Valuation).
- **Before the first sale:** create a sales agent and a walk-in customer (2 more calls/screens, see LSF-04).
- **One counter cash sale:** 4 lines → 7 calls (create, 4× add line, add payment, finalise).
- **Quotation to invoice:** 9 calls through 4 documents (quote + lines, send, accept → SO, confirm, read lines, delivery, invoice-from-delivery, then finalise).

## Top 5

1. **LSF-01:** delivery-based invoicing bills qty_base × pack price (1 crate → 25 crates, 1,437,500 TZS). This blocks wholesale crate selling.
2. **LSF-02:** the wholesale price list, customer prices and tiers never reach an invoice or POS line, so the bar is charged retail.
3. **LSF-03:** crate barcodes from Product Master lose their unit, so a scanned crate at the POS sells as one bottle.
4. **LSF-05 / LSF-17:** a counter sale cannot be voided or returned (only a money-only credit note), and abandoned drafts cannot be removed.
5. **LSF-06 / LSF-07:** money tracking is unreliable. M-Pesa and card are booked as cash, the cash book never sees sales, and the bar's credit limit is bypassed with no warning to the owner.
