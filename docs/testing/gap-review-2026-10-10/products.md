## Top 5 for this area
1. **PRD-01 + PRD-02**: retail/wholesale and customer prices don't work at the till or on invoices. The first-created price row is charged to everyone, and archived lists, the default flag and effective dates are ignored. This is a direct revenue leak for Kilimanjaro.
2. **PRD-03 + PRD-04 + PRD-05**: the Product Master looks complete but silently discards category, brand, image and serial/lot tracking. Editing on the detail screen wipes reorder levels, supplier and the 18+ flag. These are mostly one-line request-record fixes with no schema change.
3. **PRD-06**: crate barcodes can't be tied to the CRATE unit from the web (a field-name mismatch), so scanning a crate rings one bottle.
4. **PRD-08 + PRD-10 + PRD-16 + PRD-12**: the go-live Excel path mangles typed barcodes, has no pack-size sheet, needs three chained sheets, and duplicates customers on re-upload.
5. **PRD-07**: opening stock from Product Master is posted at zero value, so stock-value and profit reports are wrong from day one.
