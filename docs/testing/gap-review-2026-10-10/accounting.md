## Top 5 for this area

1. **ACC-01 + ACC-09: the fiscal-year cliff.** Nothing opens FY2027, and a mistyped overlapping year breaks every posting. From 1 Jan, sales stop reaching the books, and receipts and adjustments show accountant errors to cashiers. This is the cheapest fix with the biggest blast radius, and it lands in about 80 days.
2. **ACC-02: automatic GL failures are invisible and cannot be recovered.** There is no exceptions list, no re-post and no sales-to-GL tie-out. Every other bug here (closed period, UTC dates, overlap, TZS) turns into silently missing revenue, VAT and COGS.
3. **ACC-07: tax and statutory liabilities can never be paid down in the books.** VAT Due, WHT, PAYE, NSSF and SDL are locked against manual or cash entries, with no "record payment" action. Every year-end balance sheet is wrong.
4. **ACC-06 (+ACC-24): the VAT return does not tie to the ledger.** Credit notes, returns, debit notes and voids are ignored, and adjustments are not posted. TRA is overpaid, and 2200 and 1400 never clear.
5. **ACC-03 / ACC-04 / ACC-05: cash-sale postings are wrong at the source.** Dates are in UTC (after-midnight sales go to the previous month), part-paid credit sales lose the deposit, and M-Pesa and card all hit Cash. The cash, AR and VAT figures a bookkeeper reconciles first are all off.
