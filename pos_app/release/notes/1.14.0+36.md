**Needs the matching server update first.** Update the server, then the tills. On an older
server the till works as before (every item at every branch).

**Each branch can have its own menu and prices.** A company with several branches can now sell
some items at one branch only, and charge a different price for an item at each branch. A manager
sets this per item in Product Master (Branch availability: **Sold here** / **Price here**) or in
OrbixHQ (Edit item). Items nobody has set up are sold everywhere at the company price, as before.

- The till shows only its own branch's items: search, department buttons, the restaurant menu,
  barcode scans and the saved offline menu.
- Prices on the till are this branch's prices; the sale is charged exactly what the till shows.
- An item that is not sold here is refused with "… is not sold at this branch." — at the till,
  when scanning, and when adding it to a restaurant order. Bills for orders already placed are
  never blocked by a menu change.
- The saved offline menu, prices and stock hints belong to one branch. Signing in at another
  branch never shows them; the till refreshes them for the new branch.

Includes 1.13.0 and earlier.

**Before rolling out,** on one till at a company with two branches:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.14.0+36;
- in the ERP, give an item a different price at this branch: the till shows that price, and the
  receipt charges it;
- switch another item off for this branch: it disappears from search and its barcode says "This
  item is not sold at this branch."; the other branch still sells it.
