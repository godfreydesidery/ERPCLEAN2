**Selling by carton, box or any other pack**, not just single pieces:

- Scanning a pack barcode adds the line in that pack.
- Tapping the unit on a line switches between pieces and any pack set up for that item in the
  ERP.

A pack with its own price in the ERP sells at that price; otherwise at the piece price times
the pieces in the pack. Stock is always counted in pieces, so 2 cartons of 24 needs 48 pieces
on hand.

**HTTPS:** the till can connect to a server that uses its own (self-signed) certificate, by
trusting the certificate file in a `certs` folder next to `pos_app.exe`. See the README,
section 6.
