**Needs the server update with offline selling (V114) first.** Update the server, then the tills.

**Sell while the internet is down.** A till that a manager allows to sell offline (ERP: POS > Tills,
"Offline selling" switch - every till starts with it OFF) keeps taking cash, mobile money and card
sales when it cannot reach the server, and sends them by itself when the server is back.

- The till offers "No connection - switch to offline?" when the server cannot be reached. On a slow
  but working line a supervisor can switch it by hand, with their password.
- Offline sales go to the walk-in customer at the prices the till last received. Mobile money and
  card need their reference. No discounts, complimentary, refunds, credit or restaurant orders
  offline.
- Every offline sale is saved on the till BEFORE its receipt prints. The receipt says
  *** OFFLINE SALE *** and carries a number like OFF-T2K7-000153.
- After a restart during an outage, anyone who has signed in on that till before can sign in
  offline with their password or PIN and carry on in the same shift.
- A shift cannot be closed while its offline sales are still waiting. After 24 hours offline the
  till stops selling until it has sent its sales.
- The ERP records each offline sale once, at the price paid and the time it was sold. Managers see
  anything unusual (different price, stock below zero, below cost, held sales) in
  POS > Offline Sales Review.

See the new chapter "Selling Offline" in the user manual.

Includes 1.11.4 and earlier.

**Before rolling out,** on one till:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.12.0+33;
- in the ERP, switch Offline selling ON for that till only;
- unplug the network: accept "switch to offline", ring a cash sale and a mobile money sale with a
  reference, check both receipts say OFFLINE;
- restart the till with the network still unplugged: sign in offline and ring one more sale;
- plug the network back: sign in when asked, open Offline sales and check the waiting count goes
  to 0; check the three sales appear in the ERP and in POS > Offline Sales Review if flagged.
