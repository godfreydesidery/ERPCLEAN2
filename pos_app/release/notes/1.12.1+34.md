**Kitchen items no longer print at the barista by mistake.** An item's order slip prints where its
department is set to print, not where its name suggests. A department that was never linked to a
station (for example one typed as "Kitchen") printed at the default station.

- Add a product (on the till, for staff allowed to manage products): the Department buttons now
  show where each one prints, for example "Juices - Barista".
- A NEW department must be given a station under "Prints at" before the product can be added.
- The till does not change products or move departments. That is done in the ERP:
  Point of Sale > Restaurant Setup ("What prints where" moves a department to another station,
  "Items and their departments" moves items to another department, one or many at once), or
  Department and Prints at on the product's page under Products.
- An item's department is the same in every branch; where a department prints is set per branch.

Shops without kitchen or bar stations see no change.

Includes 1.12.0 and earlier.

**Before rolling out,** on one till:

  and Send: the slip must print at the kitchen;
- in the ERP, Restaurant Setup > Items and their departments: check the new item shows
  "Kitchen" under Prints at.
