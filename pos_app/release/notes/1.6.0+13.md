**Needs the ERP server at version 1.11.0 or later. Update the server first.**

**Each customer is charged their own price at the till.** When you choose a customer, the till
re-reads the prices of everything in the basket for that customer: their own contract prices,
then their price list, then the shop's normal prices. The total you read out is the total they
are charged. Before, the till always showed the normal price, and a sale to an account customer
on a higher price list could be refused at payment as "tenders don't cover the total".

**Every sale starts on the walk-in customer.** After each sale the till goes back to the walk-in
customer by itself, so the last customer's name and prices are never carried onto the next
shopper's sale. If no walk-in customer is set up, the till asks you to pick a customer; it no
longer quietly puts the sale on a real customer's account.

**Discounted totals match the receipt.** On a line with a discount, the total on screen used to
differ slightly from the printed receipt. The till now takes the discount off exactly as the
server does, so a customer paying the exact amount by mobile money pays the right figure.

**No more Refund cash payout.** The Cash payout box no longer offers "Refund". To give a
customer their money back, open the sale in Today's sales and use Refund / reverse: it needs a
manager's approval, and it puts the stock back and corrects the sales and VAT figures. A return
the till cannot reverse (for example from a shift that is already closed) goes to the back
office as a sales return.

**A manager approves cash leaving the drawer.** Every Cash payout and every Till expense now
asks for a manager's username and password before it is recorded, and the manager's name is
kept with it. A supervisor or branch manager working a till themselves is not asked.

**Blind cash-up.** A cashier no longer sees the Expected cash on the X-read, or the Expected and
Variance figures after closing the session. Count the drawer and enter what you counted; the
supervisor sees the result when they reconcile. Supervisors, and an X-read a manager approved
at the till, still show the expected cash.

**Hold and Recall** (supermarket register). Press Hold to put a basket aside while a customer
fetches money or another item, and serve the next person. Press Recall to bring it back; the
prices are read again from the ERP. Held sales stay on that till, for that shift, for at most a
day. Nothing is charged and no stock moves until you recall the basket and take payment.

**"Can't reach the ERP" instead of "No match".** If the network drops for a moment while you
scan or search, the till now says "Can't reach the ERP - check the connection." It used to say
"No match", as if the product did not exist.

**Price changes reach the till within minutes.** The prices in the search list are refreshed
after every sale and at least every five minutes. Before, a price changed in the office only
showed in that list after the till was restarted.

**Tills still on 1.5.x** keep selling against server 1.11.0, but:

- a Refund cash payout is refused unless the person signed in is a supervisor;
- cashiers see "Expected cash 0.00" on the X-read, because the server no longer sends them the
  figure (it is not a shortage - count the drawer as normal);
- a cash payout or till expense is recorded without a manager's approval;
- the screen shows the normal price for an account customer, who is charged their own.

Update every till to 1.6.0 together with the server.

**Before rolling out:** update the ERP server to 1.11.0. Then, on one till:

- check the version under *Server setup* reads 1.6.0+13;
- ring a basket, choose an account customer who has their own prices, and check the prices
  change; complete the sale and check the next basket is back on the walk-in customer;
- ring a line with a discount and check the screen total matches the printed receipt;
- record a small Cash payout and check a manager is asked to approve it;
- as a cashier, open the X-read and check there is no Expected cash line;
- Hold a basket, ring another sale, then Recall the first one.
