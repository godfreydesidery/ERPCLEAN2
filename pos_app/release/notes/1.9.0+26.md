**Needs the matching ERP server update** (complimentary payments, discounts and completed bills are
new on the server too). Update the server first, then the tills.

**Kitchen and bar slips no longer print twice.** On a till that both sends orders and prints "ALL
slips here, from every till", a slip could be printed a second time while the first copy was still
printing (most often on Bluetooth printers). Fixed: each slip prints once.

**Complimentary.** A new payment button, Complimentary, for food or drinks given away (the owner,
a guest, staff). Nothing is paid and no money is expected in the drawer; the sale and the stock are
recorded as usual and the cost goes to a "Complimentary Expense" account in the ERP. A cashier who
may not approve discounts is asked for a manager's name and password first. Complimentary can also
cover only part of a bill, with the rest paid in cash, card or mobile.

**Restaurant discount.** On the payment screen of a restaurant order there is now a Discount line:
tap Add, choose Percent or Amount, and the amount due changes at once. If the company's discount
limit needs a manager, the till asks for one when you complete the sale.

**Split payment** (already available): choose Cash, type the cash part, tap Add tender; choose
Card or Mobile, type that part, tap Add tender; then Complete sale.

**Orders board: Pending | Completed today.** On Floor > Orders, Pending shows the open orders as
before; Completed today shows the bills paid today, with the receipt number and the amount paid.
Tap one to see or reprint its receipt.
