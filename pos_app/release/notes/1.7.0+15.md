**Needs the ERP server with restaurant orders (the server build delivered with this version).
Update the server first.** With an older server the Restaurant register says "This ERP server
does not support restaurant orders yet"; the Supermarket and Pharmacy registers work as before.

**Prices on the restaurant menu.** The menu tiles now show each item's price, VAT included,
instead of the item code.

**A full restaurant flow: order, kitchen and bar slips, bill, then payment.**

- Orders are kept on the ERP, not on the till. Many tables can be open at once, every till and
  waiter tablet sees the same orders, and nothing is lost if a till restarts.
- **Send** prints a slip at each station that makes something on the order: food to the
  kitchen, drinks to the bar or barista. Only new items are sent each time.
- **Bill** prints the bill on the cashier's receipt printer only. **PAY** takes payment as
  usual and closes the order; the bill can be split evenly (2, 3 or 4 ways) or by item.
- Items can carry choices ("No sugar", "Extra cheese" - priced extras appear on the bill),
  a note for the kitchen, and a course: hold the mains and **Fire** them when the table is ready.
- A sent item is voided with a reason (a manager approves if the cashier may not), and a VOID
  slip tells the kitchen to stop.
- Move an order to another table, merge two orders, or cancel an order from the **More** menu.

**Waiters.** Waiters can work on their own tablet or phone (the new Waiter role: they order,
send and print bills; the cashier takes payment), or share the cashier's till: turn on
"Shared till: ask who is serving" in the restaurant Settings, and each waiter signs in with
their own username before taking an order. Their name is printed on the slips and the bill.

**Any kitchen or bar printer works.** In the restaurant Settings on each till:

- a network printer (cable or WiFi) set up in the ERP prints from every till directly;
- a printer plugged into a PC by USB: on that PC choose the printer for the station and tick
  "Print ALL slips here" - keep OrbixPOS open there and it prints for every till;
- no printer: the kitchen follows orders on the Kitchen Display in the ERP.

**Before rolling out,** a manager sets up the restaurant once in the ERP
(Point of Sale -> Restaurant Setup): tables, the kitchen and bar stations with their printers,
and which item categories print where (for example "Drinks" to the bar). Items without a
category print at the kitchen. Then, on one till:

- check the version under *Server setup* reads 1.7.0+15;
- open the Restaurant register, open a table, add a food item and a drink and tap **Send**:
  one slip prints in the kitchen and one at the bar;
- tap **Bill** (the bill prints at the cashier), then **PAY** and finish the sale; the table
  is free again.

Chapter 4 of the user manual (Selling - Pharmacy and Restaurant) describes every screen.
