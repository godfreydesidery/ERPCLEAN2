**Printing to Bluetooth printers on Android fixed.** On some Android devices every print failed
with "Could not print to the Bluetooth printer", even with the printer switched on and paired
(seen on an iMin Swan 2 with ZJ80UB printers). Two causes, both fixed:

- on Android 12 and later the app tripped over a Bluetooth permission it does not need before it
  even reached the printer;
- many printers (ZJiang ZJ80 family, some built-in "InnerPrinter" services) refuse the standard
  secure Bluetooth connection. The app now tries the other usual ways to connect, remembers the
  one that works for each printer, and sends the receipt in small pieces so a small printer
  buffer does not drop the end of it.

The message now says which part failed: "Could not reach the printer" (off, out of range, not
paired, or connected to another phone or till - a Bluetooth printer takes one device at a time)
or "The printer connected but did not take the receipt" (paper, cover).

On Android, the printer list in Server setup now explains what to do when it is empty.

Works with the same ERP server as 1.7.4. Nothing changes on Windows.

**Before rolling out,** on the Android device:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.7.5+20;
- open Server setup, choose the printer and press Test print - try each paired printer
  (InnerPrinter and each ZJ80UB) and keep the one that prints;
- for a restaurant, pick the kitchen and bar printers in Restaurant > Settings and press Test.
