**The till shows when it is waiting on a slow connection.** On a weak line a request can take up
to about 45 seconds (15 to connect, 30 for the answer), and the till used to just grey out its
buttons - it looked frozen, so staff tapped again or restarted the app.

- **Complete sale** turns into a spinner reading "Recording the sale..." while the ERP records it.
- **Send, Fire and Bill** (restaurant) show a spinner on the button pressed, until the slips or the
  bill have printed.
- After 5 seconds of waiting, a line appears: "Slow connection - still waiting for the ERP...". It
  shows on the payment screen, the restaurant screens and under the supermarket search box, and
  goes away by itself when the answer arrives. Wait for it - do not tap again or close the app.

Includes 1.7.8+23 (kitchen and bar slips in large type). Works with the same ERP server as 1.7.7.

**Before rolling out,** on the counter till:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.7.9+24;
- ring a sale and press Complete sale: the button shows a spinner until the receipt appears;
- send a restaurant order: the Send button spins until the slips have printed.
