**The waiter chosen with "Who's serving?" now serves the order.** On a till set to "Don't ask"
(Restaurant > Settings > Who is serving?), a cashier could tap "Who's serving?", sign a waiter in
and see "Serving: <waiter>", yet new orders were still opened in the cashier's name - so the
bill and the receipt showed the cashier as the waiter (seen at Cafe Tulia: waiter samwel, bill
said Mr Issa).

Now the signed-in waiter is recorded as serving every order opened while they are signed in, on
"Don't ask" just as on "Ask before a new order". With nobody signed in, the cashier serves, as
before. The waiter signs out by tapping the x on "Serving: <waiter>", or after 3 idle minutes.

Orders opened before this update keep the waiter they were opened with. To move one, open it and
use More > Transfer to another waiter.

Works with the same ERP server as 1.7.6. Includes the 1.7.5 and 1.7.6 printing fixes.

**Before rolling out,** on the counter till:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.7.7+22;
- tap "Who's serving?", sign a waiter in, open a table, add an item and print the bill: the bill
  says "Served by" that waiter;
- take the payment: the receipt says "WAITER:" that waiter and "CASHIER:" the cashier.
