**Barcode scanning:**

- After a scan the item is added and the search list closes. Before, the list re-opened a
  moment later showing the item just scanned.
- While that list was open, the NEXT scan added the previous item again instead of the one
  scanned. Each scan now adds the item scanned.

Typing to search, the arrow keys and Enter work as before.

**Sales the server could not confirm:** when the ERP's answer to a sale is unclear, the till
still asks you to retry, and now also shows the reason the ERP gave.

**Prices:** an item priced only in its base unit could ring up at 0.00. It now rings at its
price.

**Before rolling out:** on one till, scan three different items quickly one after another and
check the basket holds exactly those three, once each.
