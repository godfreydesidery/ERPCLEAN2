**New receipt layout**, in the style of a supermarket tax receipt:

- a customer block, then RECEIPT NO, RECEIPT DATE, RECEIPT TIME and CASHIER;
- an item table with Description, Qty and Amount, and a tax letter on every line:
  **A** = standard rate (18%), **C** = zero-rated, **E** = exempt;
- TOTAL EXCL OF TAX, TAX A-18%, TOTAL TAX and TOTAL INCL OF TAX;
- payments spelled out in full (CASH, MOBILE MONEY, CARD, CHEQUE) and the change.

It is an ordinary sales receipt, **not** a TRA fiscal (EFD/VFD) receipt: it has no TRA
verification code or QR code.

Also:

- The "Sale complete" screen shows exactly what the printer prints.
- A sale with no customer name prints "n/a" (it used to print an internal customer number).
- A refund prints the name of the manager who approved it.
- Very large amounts no longer run off a 58 mm receipt.

**Before rolling out:** on one till, print a receipt on the paper you use (58 mm or 80 mm) and
check the amounts line up on the right edge.
