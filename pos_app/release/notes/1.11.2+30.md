**No server update needed.** Works with the ERP server that 1.11.0 needs.

**Managers see sales without opening a shift.** The Open shift screen (where you choose a till)
now has two buttons under the company and branch name: **Sales dashboard** and **Today's sales**.
They open straight away - no till to choose, no shift to open. Before, a manager had to open a
shift just to look, which started a real cash session that someone then had to close and
reconcile. Only users allowed to view sales invoices see the buttons; cashiers see no change.
Both are still in the session menu during a shift as before.

Includes 1.11.1 (Split bill).

**Before rolling out,** on one till:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.11.2+30;
- sign in as a manager and, without choosing a till, open Sales dashboard and Today's sales;
- sign in as a cashier and check the two buttons are not shown.
