**Restaurant: an Orders board.** The Floor screen has a new switch, **Tables | Orders**. Orders
shows every open order (tables and takeaways) as a card, so you can see what is on each order
without opening it:

- at the top: the table or takeaway, the order number, how long it has been open (for example
  "51m") and the waiter; "BILL PRINTED" when the bill is out;
- in the middle: every item with its quantity and price, add-ons under their item. Items not yet
  sent to the kitchen or bar are marked NEW - so what a guest has just added stands out. HELD
  items wait for their course; READY items are done in the kitchen. Voided items are not shown;
- at the bottom: the total - the bill total once the bill is printed, otherwise the running
  total at menu prices.

Tap a card to open the order. When several waiters have open orders, choose one to see only theirs.
The longest-open orders come first.

The Floor screen (Tables and Orders) now refreshes itself every 20 seconds, so orders opened or
added to on other devices appear without pressing Refresh.

Works with the same ERP server as 1.7.9. Includes 1.7.8 (large kitchen and bar slips) and 1.7.9
(slow-connection feedback).

**Before rolling out,** on the counter till:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.8.0+25;
- open a table, add two items and Send, then add one more: on Floor > Orders the card shows the
  three items, the last one marked NEW;
- print the bill: the card shows BILL PRINTED and the bill total.
