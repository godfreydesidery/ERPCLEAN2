**Every screen size.** OrbixPOS now fits phones, tablets, Android POS devices and every common
till screen (1024x768, 1280x1024, 1366x768, 1920x1080, including Windows set to 125%). No button,
column or total is cut off any more. Works with the same ERP server as 1.7.3 (no server update
needed).

**On a phone** (or a tablet held upright):

- the supermarket register is one column: search at the top, the basket as cards with quantity,
  unit, discount, remove and void on each line, and customer, Hold, Recall and PAY at the bottom;
- the number pad opens with the keypad button (or by tapping a line's quantity or discount) and
  folds away again;
- the mode switch at the top is a dropdown showing the current mode;
- the payment screen is one column with Complete sale always in view;
- the pharmacy register puts the total and PAY under the lines;
- the open-shift screen stacks the modes and puts Open session full width.

A phone held sideways shows the controls beside the basket instead.

**On a 1024x768 till** the basket columns tighten (the item code moves under the name) and the
number pad's bottom row (".", "0", delete), which used to be cut off, shows in full.

**On Android** the system keyboard no longer pops up over the basket after every scan. The scanner
still types into the search box; tap the box to bring up the keyboard and type a name. Quantity,
discount and payment amounts use the on-screen keypads.

**Also fixed:** two-line dish names no longer overflow their menu tile, and the restaurant Settings
printer row fits on a phone.

**Before rolling out,** on one Android device and one Windows till:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.7.4+19;
- ring a sale with the number pad (change a quantity) and pay it;
- on the Android device, scan an item and check the keyboard stays down.
