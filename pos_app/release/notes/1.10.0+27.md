**Waiters can sign in with a 4-digit PIN.** On the sign-in screen a waiter taps their name and, if
they have a PIN, a number pad opens: four digits and they are in. Waiters without a PIN still type
their password, and "Use password instead" is always there. The same PIN works in "Who's serving?"
on a shared till.

- **Waiters only.** Cashiers and managers always sign in with their password; a PIN can never
  approve anything a manager approves.
- **Setting a PIN:** a manager sets, resets or removes it in the ERP (Users > the user > POS PIN). A
  waiter can change their own in the session menu (Change my PIN), confirming with their current PIN
  or password.
- **Easy PINs are refused** (1111, 1234, 4321 and the like). **Five wrong PINs lock the waiter for
  15 minutes**, the same rule as passwords.
- A new PIN shows on a till's name tiles after the next sign-in on that till (the tiles refresh
  then).
