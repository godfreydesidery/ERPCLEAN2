**No more silent "Sent" when a kitchen or bar slip did not print.** When a till sends an order to
a station that has no printer chosen on that till (and no network printer in the ERP), the slip is
not printed there. Until now the till still said "Sent to KITCHEN and BARISTA." in green, so it
looked as if both slips had printed while the kitchen got nothing.

Now the till says which station was not printed and what to do, for example: "Sent, but BARISTA
was not printed: no printer is chosen for it on this till. Choose one in Restaurant > Settings,
then reprint."

A waiter's own phone or tablet, which relies on a kitchen till printing slips for every device,
still just says "Sent".

Works with the same ERP server as 1.7.5. Includes the Android Bluetooth printing fix of 1.7.5.

**Before rolling out,** on the counter till:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.7.6+21;
- in Restaurant > Settings > Kitchen & bar printers, choose a printer for every station and press
  Test on each;
- send one order with a food item and a drink and check both slips come out at the right place.
