**Works with the same ERP server as 1.6.0 (version 1.11.0 or later).** No server update is
needed for this version.

**No more "Your session ended" when the ERP is slow or restarting.** The till renews the
cashier's sign-in in the background every few minutes. If the ERP did not answer at that moment
(it was restarting, busy, or the network dropped), the till used to send the cashier back to the
sign-in screen with "Your session ended. Please sign in again." Now the cashier stays in the
till, sees "Cannot reach the ERP", and carries on as soon as the ERP answers again. The till
still signs the cashier out when the ERP really ends the session, for example after their
password was changed or their account was switched off.

**Only one OrbixPOS opens at a time.** Opening OrbixPOS while it is already running now brings
the open till to the front instead of starting a second copy. Two copies on one computer shared
one sign-in, and the ERP read that as someone stealing it and signed the cashier out
everywhere.

**One sign-in per person.** If the same username is signed in on two tills, or on a till and the
ERP in a web browser, the ERP can still end both sessions. Give each cashier their own username.

**Before rolling out,** on one till:

- check the version under *Server setup* reads 1.6.1+14;
- open OrbixPOS, then open it again from the desktop icon: the open till comes to the front and
  no second window appears;
- sign in, ring a sale and take payment as normal.
