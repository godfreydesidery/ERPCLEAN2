**Needs the matching server update first.** Update the server, then the tills. On an older
server the till works exactly as before.

**The Z-read shows the shift's sales.** After a session is reconciled, the Z-read (on screen and
on paper) still starts with everything it showed before - sales by tender, the cash in the
drawer, the variance - and now adds, underneath:

- **Summary** - gross sales, discounts, net sales, the VAT inside them and the number of
  transactions;
- **Categories** - sales per item category;
- **Departments** - sales per kitchen or bar station the items were sent to ("Unassigned" for
  items never sent to a station, such as counter sales);
- **Team members** - sales per waiter (or the cashier, for counter sales), with how many sales
  each one made.

Every section adds up to the shift's total sales. An empty section is left out, and every line
fits 58 mm and 80 mm paper.

Includes 1.14.0 and earlier.

- reconcile (or reprint the Z-read of) a shift and print it on the shop's own printer: the new
  sections print after the drawer figures and before the signature lines, nothing is cut off.
