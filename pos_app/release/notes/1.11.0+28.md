**Needs the matching ERP server update** (categories and the dashboard are new on the server).
Update the server first, then the tills. Split payment and the complimentary name work on the old
server too.

**Split payment works the way cashiers expect.** Pay part in cash and the rest by mobile money or
card: after each part is added, what is still to pay is filled in for the next one, and **Complete
sale** counts the amount still in the box. A **Remaining** line shows what is left to pay.

**Complimentary receipts say who it was for.** Choosing Complimentary asks "Given to" (required);
the receipt and every reprint print "Given to: <name>".

**Browse by category.** A row of category buttons (the product's department) sits on the
restaurant menu and under the search on the shop screen. Tap one to see just those items; tap it
again, or All, to go back. Shops with no categories see no change.

**Add a product from the till.** Session menu > Add a product: name, selling price, department,
unit, VAT, track stock, and an optional barcode and code. Only users allowed to manage products in
the ERP (managers, admins) see it. A new department also needs routing to the kitchen or bar in the
ERP (Restaurant set-up), or its slips print at the default station.

**Sales dashboard.** Session menu > Sales dashboard: this branch's sales for today, yesterday, the
last 7 days, this month or chosen dates — the total, number of sales and average sale, sales per
day, per payment type, and per department with the items sold in each. No cost or profit is shown.
Users who can view sales invoices see it.
