**Needs the matching server update first.** Update the server, then the tills. On an older
server the new Return items button reports that the server does not support it.

**Refund by item.** A customer can now return some of the items on a receipt instead of the
whole sale. Until now a refund reversed everything on the receipt.

- Open the receipt (Session menu > Today's sales, or Recent receipts) and tap **Return items**.
- Tick the items being returned and set how many of each. The screen shows, per item, how many
  were sold, how many are already returned and how many are left; you cannot return more than
  is left.
- Type the reason. A cashier needs a manager to approve, exactly as for Refund / reverse; a
  supervisor can approve their own.
- The till then shows how much cash to give the customer and prints a REFUND slip. The refund
  is always paid in cash from the drawer, whatever the customer paid with, and the expected cash
  at close drops by the same amount.
- Items go back into stock at their original cost. Revenue and VAT are reduced for the returned
  items only. One item returned from a line refunds exactly its shelf price.
- Once any item on a receipt has been returned, **Refund / reverse** is no longer offered for
  it; return the remaining items with Return items instead. The receipt lists its returns, each
  with a Reprint slip button.
- If the network drops while a return is being sent, the till says "Do not hand over any cash
  yet" and offers Retry. Retrying can never refund twice.
- Returns need the till to be online, and the shift that rang the sale must still be open.
  Complimentary and credit (unpaid) sales cannot be returned at the till.
- The REFUND slip is not a fiscal receipt.

**Add a product:** the hint under "Track stock" now reads "Turn off for services or items made
to order" (it used to mention dishes only). Behaviour is unchanged.

Includes 1.12.1 and earlier.
