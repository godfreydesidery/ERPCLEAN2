**Needs the ERP server delivered with this version** (it adds the waiter list and the waiter
reports). Update the server first. With the 1.7.0 server the till works as before, but shows no
waiter tiles.

**Waiters sign in by tapping their name.** The sign-in screen shows the branch's waiters as
tiles. A waiter taps their name, then types their password on the on-screen keyboard, which
comes up once a name is tapped. With many waiters there is a search box and an A-Z strip: tap a
letter to see only the names starting with it. The same tiles appear in "Who's serving?" on a
shared till. The till keeps the list from the last time someone signed in on it, so the tiles
are there while nobody is signed in; a new waiter appears after the next sign-in.

**Every table shows its waiter.** Occupied tables show who is serving them. The floor lists
every open order - tables and takeaways - with its waiter, item count and bill amount, and can
be filtered by waiter, so the cashier finds a bill quickly. The order screen and the payment
screen also name the waiter.

**The receipt names the waiter and the table.** A restaurant receipt prints WAITER and
TABLE / ORDER under the cashier. To print the shop's address, phone and email at the top, fill
them in once in the ERP (Administration -> Company); the receipt already prints them.

**The 0 key is back on the payment screen.** The bottom row of the number pad (C, 0, backspace)
was pushed off the screen by the "Split evenly" buttons. The keys now always fit.

**Restaurant-only till.** A manager can switch on "Restaurant only - this till" in the
restaurant Settings. That till then shows only the Restaurant register; Supermarket and
Pharmacy are hidden for everyone who signs in on it.

**Server setup is hidden.** So nobody changes the server or printer by accident, the Server
setup link no longer shows on the sign-in screen. To open it, tap the version text at the bottom
of the sign-in screen seven times quickly (the till counts down from the fourth tap). If the
till cannot reach the server at all, the link shows by itself.

**Shared till:** a waiter who signed in on the till itself is signed out after 3 minutes without
use, so the next waiter finds the tiles. Waiters also have a Sign out button on the floor.

**In the ERP (web)**, Point of Sale -> Restaurant Reports: sales by waiter, bills paid (with
waiter, cashier, receipt and how it was paid) and pending bills (open, or printed and not yet
paid), for one waiter or all, with PDF, Excel and CSV export. Give each waiter the "Waiter" role
in the ERP so they get a tile.

**Before rolling out,** on one till:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.7.1+16;
- sign in once as the cashier, sign out: the waiter tiles appear;
- tap a waiter, type their password, open a table and send an order: the table shows the
  waiter's name;
- bill and pay it: the 0 key is visible, and the receipt shows WAITER and TABLE / ORDER.
