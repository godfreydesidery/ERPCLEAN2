**Kitchen and bar slips print in large type.** The station name, the table (or TAKEAWAY), every
item, its add-ons and notes now print at double size, readable from across the kitchen or the
bar. The order number, time, round, guests and who sent it stay normal size. A void slip's
"VOID - DO NOT MAKE" prints large too.

**Bills and receipts are unchanged** - normal size, as before.

Large type fits half as many letters per line (24 on 80 mm paper, 16 on 58 mm), so a long item
name continues on the next line.

Works with the same ERP server as 1.7.7. Includes the earlier printing and waiter fixes.

**Before rolling out,** on the counter till:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.7.8+23;
- send one order with a food item and a drink: both slips print with large items;
- print the bill and take the payment: bill and receipt are normal size.
