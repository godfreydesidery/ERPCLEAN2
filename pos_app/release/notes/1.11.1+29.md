**No server update needed.** Works with the ERP server that 1.11.0 needs.

**Split bill.** The payment screen has a **Split bill** button (it used to say "Split evenly", which
was only a label and did nothing when tapped). Tap it and enter how much is paid each way: Cash,
Mobile, Cheque, Card or Complimentary. For a bill of 35,000: 10,000 cash, 15,000 card, then tap
**Rest** beside Mobile to put the remaining 10,000 there. A **Remaining** line shows what is still
to pay, and **Complete sale** only works once the whole bill is covered. The sale then completes
and the receipt comes up, ready to print, listing each payment.

Only cash gives change, so cash may be more than what is left; the other types together may not be
more than the bill. A complimentary part still asks "Given to" and needs a manager as before.

On restaurant orders the 2 / 3 / 4 buttons beside Split bill still key in an equal share for each
guest.

**Before rolling out,** on the counter till:

- check the version at the bottom of the sign-in screen reads 1.11.1+29;
- on a table bill, tap Split bill, enter part cash, part card, and Rest on Mobile, then Complete
  sale, and check the receipt shows all three payments;
- check the X-read shows each amount under its payment type.
