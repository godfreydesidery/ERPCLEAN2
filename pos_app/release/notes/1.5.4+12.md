**Needs the ERP server at version 1.10.2 or later.** Update the server first. On an older
server the till still sells, but Today's sales falls back to the old list, and the reprint and
refund improvements below do not apply.

**Sales are credited to the right person.** A user who could see the sales-agent list (a Sales
Manager, for example) had every sale credited to whichever agent happened to come first in that
list. Every sale is now credited to the person signed in at the till. A cashier with no
sales-agent record gets one automatically on their first sale.

**A manager can print their own Z-read.** Printing the Z-read used to need a second manager,
even for the Branch Manager who had just reconciled it, so a shop with one manager on duty could
not print it at the till. A manager signed in now prints it directly; a cashier still needs a
manager to approve.

**Refunds:** the *Refund / reverse* button now appears only on sales you may refund. A cashier
sees it on their own shift's sales; a supervisor on any sale. Before, it appeared on a colleague's
sale, the manager approved, and only then was the refund refused.

**Reprints name the right cashier.** The CASHIER line now shows who rang the sale, not whoever is
reprinting it.

**The cash drawer opens only for the sale itself:** on the first print of the sale's receipt.
Never on a reprint, a gift receipt, a reversed sale or a second copy.

**Today's sales means today, at this branch:** till sales since midnight, newest first, with the
time and the cashier. Reversed sales stay in the list, marked *Reversed*.

**Age-restricted items:** *Cancel* on the age check now always stops the sale. A user allowed to
override the age check gets a separate *Override without check* button.

**New: Till expense.** Record cash paid out for the business (transport, cleaning, repairs and so
on) from the Session menu, with a category. It is booked to the ledger under that category and
shows as its own line on the X-read and Z-read.

**The version is on screen:** under *Server setup* on the sign-in screen and at the bottom of the
Session menu.

**Before rolling out:** update the ERP server to 1.10.2. On one till, ring a cash sale and print
it (the drawer should open), reprint it from Today's sales (the drawer should stay shut and the
CASHIER line should name the cashier), and check the version under *Server setup*.
