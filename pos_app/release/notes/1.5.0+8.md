**Drawer reports with manager approval:**

- A cashier allowed to see their drawer opens the X-read (mid-shift report) with no prompt.
- A cashier who is not allowed can still open it when a manager types their own username and
  password at the till.
- Printing or reprinting the Z-read (end-of-shift statement) always needs a manager.
